- **Antenna:** the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1**U** has no PCB antenna - Wi-Fi/Bluetooth will not work without an antenna. Mount the SMA bulkhead of A1 in the enclosure wall (hole approx. 6.5 mm), plug the IPEX end into the u.FL socket of U4 and screw the antenna A2 onto the SMA connector; the antenna can be folded/swiveled to point upwards. Use standard SMA (not RP-SMA) parts so the antenna and cable fit together. Alternative foldable antenna with higher gain (195 mm, 8 dBi): BAT WIRELESS BW2.4JWX195-13KJ ([C497018](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C497018.html)). Other cable lengths of the same series: KH-IPEX-SMA-W-120 ([C2683342](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C2683342.html)), KH-IPEX-SMA-W-200 ([C2683345](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C2683345.html)).
- **Ordering with JLCPCB:** A1 and A2 can be ordered at LCSC and shipped together with the assembled boards (combine the LCSC order with the JLCPCB order at checkout).
- **Mating cables A4/A5:** ready-made XH cables with 150 mm wires are not a standard LCSC stock item. Order them through the LCSC Custom Cables service ([lcsc.com/customcables](https://lcsc.com/customcables)): minimum order 1 piece, quotation within 24 hours. Specify per cable: housing and contacts as listed above, wire UL1007 24 AWG, length 150 mm, all positions populated, other end stripped and tinned, quantity = number of boards. Pin 1 of the housing must match pin 1 of J3/J4 (J4: 1 GND, 2 +19 V, 3 UART1 TX, 4 UART1 RX, 5 UART2 TX, 6 UART2 RX; J3: 1 VIN 19 V, 2 +3V3, 3 +5V, 4 VBUS, 5 RTC_INT, 6 I2C_SDA, 7 I2C_SCL, 8 IO42, 9 IO41, 10 IO40, 11 IO39, 12 IO38, 13/14 GND). Custom cable orders can be combined with the LCSC/JLCPCB shipment. Off-the-shelf alternative for J4 only (200 mm instead of 150 mm): BXCONN XH6P-24AWG-200-A1T ([C53413416](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C53413416.html)).
- **CR2032:** lithium coin cells are dangerous goods for shipping and are often not sent internationally - buy them locally. Insert with "+" facing up.

## Notes

- **LED chain:** LED1 (status) and LED2 (RX indicator) are WS2812B on GPIO48. J5 (XH-3A) extends the chain for LEDs mounted in the enclosure: pin 1 +5 V, pin 2 DATA, pin 3 GND. D5 lowers the supply of LED1 by about 0.3 V so that the 3.3 V data signal stays above the input-high threshold. D6 is the power-OK LED on +3V3.
- Ready-to-upload BOM files for assembly services are in the `KiCad` folder: `EBCTester_BOM_JLCPCB.csv` (JLCPCB) and `EBCTester_BOM_PCBWay.xlsx` (PCBWay).
